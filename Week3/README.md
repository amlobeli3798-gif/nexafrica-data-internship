# Week 3: Superstore Data Analysis with Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the Superstore dataset using Power BI to uncover insights on products and customers. Built two interactive dashboards: Product Analysis and Customer Analysis.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify top/bottom performing products by sales and profit
- Analyze sales, profit, and quantity by Category and Sub-Category
- Segment customers by value and understand buying behavior

## 📊 Dashboards

### 1. Product Analysis
- **Top 10 Products by Sales** and **Top 10 by Profit**
- **Bottom 10 Products by Profit** - identifying loss-makers
- **Sales by Sub-Category:** Phones and Chairs lead
- **Profit by Sub-Category:** Copiers, Phones, Accessories lead
- **Quantity by Sub-Category:** Binders and Paper highest volume
- **Profit by Category:** Technology > Office Supplies > Furniture

### 2. Customer Analysis
- **793** Total Customers
- **2.90K** Sales per Customer
- **361.16** Profit per Customer
- **6.32** Orders per Customer
- **458.61** Avg Order Value

**Customer Segmentation:**
- High-Value: 117 customers (14.75%)
- Medium-Value: 402 customers (50.69%)
- Low-Value: 274 customers (34.55%)

Medium-Value customers drive the highest total sales.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query for data cleaning
- DAX for measures (Customer Sales, Segmentation)

## 💡 Key Insights
1. Technology is most profitable, Furniture lags despite sales volume.
2. Copiers are profit champions; some products consistently lose money.
3. 14.75% of customers (High-Value) need retention focus.
4. Binders sell in high quantity but low profit - review pricing.
