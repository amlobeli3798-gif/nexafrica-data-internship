# NEXAFRICA Superstore Dashboard - Week 5 Final Project

## 📊 Project Overview
Built a 4-page interactive Excel dashboard analyzing 9,994 Superstore transactions (2014-2017).

**Key Metrics:**
- Total Sales: R2,297,201
- Total Profit: R286,397 (12.47% margin)
- Total Orders: 5,009
- Total Customers: 793
- Avg Order Value: R458

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

**Page 1 - Executive Overview**
- 7 KPI Cards (Sales, Profit, Orders, Customers, Quantity, Margin, AOV)
- Sales Trend (Line Chart Month-Year)
- Sales by Region (Bar Chart)
- Sales by Category (Donut)
- Profit by Category (Bar)

**Page 2 - Product Performance**
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Bottom 10 Products
- Category & Sub-Category analysis
- Slicers: Category, Sub-category, Region, Year

**Page 3 - Customer Analysis**
- Customer Segmentation Donut (Consumer 33%, Corporate 34%, Home Office 33%)
- Top Customer: Customer 2896 - R12,385
- Sales per Customer: R2,893
- Slicers: Segment, Region, Year

**Page 4 - Business Insights**
- 5 Data-driven Findings
- 5 Actionable Recommendations for 20% profit growth

## 🔍 Key Insights
1. **Balanced Segments:** All 3 segments equal (~33% each) - no dependency risk
2. **Technology Most Profitable:** 15.2% margin vs Furniture 11.8%
3. **Opportunity:** Increase AOV from R458 to R600 via cross-sell

## 🛠️ Tools
- Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers)
- DAX-like formulas (SUM, COUNT DISTINCT, AVERAGE)
